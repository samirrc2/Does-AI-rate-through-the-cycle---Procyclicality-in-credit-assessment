First controlled test of procyclicality in LLM credit ratings.
All five frontier LLMs downgrade identical firms as the macro worsens.
Pooled effect: 0.43 rating notches of downgrade per macro-severity step.
The effect is asymmetric: about 3x steeper into recessions than into booms.
A "through-the-cycle" prompt anchors ratings rather than de-cyclifying them.
